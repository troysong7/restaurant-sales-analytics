# Restaurant Sales & Operations Analytics

## Overview
A business analytics portfolio project exploring restaurant sales, profitability, customer demand, and operational trends. The project uses a synthetic restaurant transaction dataset containing approximately 100,000 line items across four locations over six months. The goal is to demonstrate how data can be transformed into practical business insights and recommendations.

## Business Questions
- Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products have the strongest and weakest margins?
- When are customer orders most concentrated?
- How do the four locations compare?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- What operational opportunities can be identified from the data?

## Key Findings
Analysis of the synthetic dataset identified several notable patterns:
- Friday through Sunday accounted for approximately 53% of revenue.
- The 6–10 PM period accounted for approximately 57% of daily revenue.
- Wings and entrees generated substantial revenue but had lower margins than beverages and sides.
- The Airport Rd location had similar average order value but lower overall traffic.
- Discounts reduced margins without producing a proportionally large increase in volume.

## Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis:
1. Evaluate product pricing and costs for high-volume, lower-margin items.
2. Consider staffing and inventory adjustments around peak evening and weekend demand.
3. Investigate opportunities to increase traffic at lower-volume locations.
4. Evaluate whether discount strategies are generating enough additional volume to justify their effect on margins.
